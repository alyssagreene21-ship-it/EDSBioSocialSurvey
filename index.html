About This Research

This platform extends the groundbreaking work of the Norris Lab EDS/HSD study (2024), which surveyed 3,906 validated participants and documented the profound medical complexity of hypermobile conditions.

What's Different About This Study?

While the Norris Lab provided comprehensive biomedical data, our project adds the biosocial layer:

  • Medical Stigma: Quantifying gaslighting, dismissal, and epistemic injustice
  • Social Determinants: How class, insurance, geography create access barriers
  • Intersectionality: Race, gender, sexuality's impact on diagnosis and care
  • Lived Experience: The psychological toll of invisible disability
  • Community Power: How peer support and advocacy sustain us
  • Subtype-Specific Challenges: Unique barriers faced by each EDS subtype

Data Usage & Privacy

All responses are anonymous and stored using secure browser storage. No personal identifying information is collected. Aggregated, de-identified data will be used for:

  • Public data visualizations showing patterns of stigma and access barriers
  • Advocacy materials for patient organizations
  • Educational resources for medical providers
  • Potential peer-reviewed publications (with community consent)

Built With Care

This platform was designed using trauma-informed principles, acknowledging that answering questions about medical dismissal and stigma can be emotionally difficult. Take breaks as needed.

Questions or Concerns?

This is a community research project. If you have feedback, concerns about the questions, or ideas for improvement, your input is welcome and will shape how this study evolves.
